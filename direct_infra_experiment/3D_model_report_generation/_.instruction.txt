# Report Generation: 3D Urban Scene Generation

## Objective
Generate a thorough academic-style report on **3D Urban Scene Generation** covering state-of-the-art methods, techniques, applications, and future directions.

---

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Outline & Topic Identification
- Brainstorm and identify the key areas/aspects to cover in the report
- Create a structured outline with main sections and subsections
- Define the scope and depth for each section

**Potential Areas to Explore:**
1. Introduction & Background
2. Problem Definition & Challenges
3. Data Sources & Representations (point clouds, meshes, voxels, implicit representations)
4. Traditional Methods (procedural generation, rule-based systems)
5. Deep Learning Approaches (GANs, VAEs, diffusion models, transformers)
6. Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) and Gaussian Splatting for urban scenes
7. Text-to-3D and Image-to-3D urban generation
8. Datasets & Benchmarks
9. Evaluation Metrics
10. Applications (autonomous driving, urban planning, gaming, AR/VR)
11. Current Limitations & Open Challenges
12. Future Directions

### Phase 2: Section Writing
- Research and write each identified section individually
- Include relevant citations, figures, and technical details
- Maintain consistent academic tone and formatting

### Phase 3: Integration & Finalization
- Combine all sections into a cohesive report
- Add abstract, conclusion, and references
- Review for consistency, flow, and completeness
- Final editing and polish

---

## Output Files
- `outline.md` - Initial outline and structure
- `sections/` - Individual section drafts
- `report_final.md` - Complete integrated report

---

## Notes
- Focus on recent advances (2020-2026)
- Balance between breadth (survey-style) and depth (technical details)
- Include visual diagrams/tables where appropriate
